## Frequently asked questions

### How do I charge an electric scooter?

Ather has the largest EV two-wheeler Fast charging grid network in India. There are 3 ways to charge an Ather EV Electric Scooter. The first is with the portable charger provided with your Ather scooter that can be plugged into any 5 amp socket at home, just like you charge your mobile phone. The second is through 6,100+ fast-charging grids across the country that can give you up to 15 kms range for every 10 minutes of charging. The third is at public spaces like apartment complexes, malls, university campuses and office buildings where Ather Neighbourhood charges are installed. To request for one to be installed in your building, contact us here.

### Can I use an electric scooter if I live in an apartment?

Yes. You just need a 5 amp socket at your parking slot. You can conveniently charge your Ather EV with the portable charger at all apartments with a basic electricity connection in the parking area.

### Do I need a Drivers’ License, Helmet and Registration to use an electric scooter?

An Ather electric scooter is subject to the same Central & State road safety laws as any other scooter. Therefore, yes, a valid drivers’ license, registration & helmet are absolutely mandatory for riding an Ather Electric Scooter.

### Why do Electric Scooters have riding modes?

Ather 450 Electric scooters provide different riding modes to suit the riding needs of its customers. If you need more range, then you can choose Smart ECO or Eco Mode. If you need more acceleration and higher speed, you can switch to other models such as Ride, Sport, Zip or Warp. Warp and Zip modes offer highest acceleration and top speed for 450 and Rizta line of products respectively.

### What is the cost of charging an electric scooter?

You can charge Ather Electric scooters at home using portable chargers provided with Ather electric scooters by connecting to a normal 5 amp socket that you generally use to charge your mobile phones. The cost of carging at home depends on electricity charges for your connection. Our fast charging network is also currently free to use in most places across India, except 5 states (Karnataka, Delhi, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Telangana) where we charge ₹ 1 /Min.

### Do I ever need to replace an electric scooter’s battery?

Eight70 Warranty covers your battery for 8 years or 80 000 km, whichever comes first. Under this plan, your Ather electric scooter battery will be replaced for free if the battery health falls below 70%. No questions asked.

### Can electric scooters be used in the rain?

Ather Electric scooter battery is IP67 rated, which means the battery is Water Resistant. Even the electronic dashboard is water resistant. So, whether it’s heavy rain or high heat, riding an Ather is worry free.

### What is the price of electric scooter?

The Ather Rizta electric scooter in Bangalore starts at an ex-showroom price of ₹1,14,999. The on-road price for the base variant typically begins around ₹1,22,572. The Ather 450S electric scooter starts at an ex-showroom price of ₹1,29,999. The more powerful Ather 450X electric scooter has an ex-showroom price of ₹1,46,999. Prices may vary based on specific variants, RTO charges per city, insurance, and optional add-ons. You can check detailed pricing of both the models here - Ather 450 | Ather Rizta.

### Which electric scooter is best in India with price?

The Ather electric scooter is the best electric scooter in Bangalore prices starting from ₹1,22,572. Ather scooters feature advanced capabilities like integrated Google Maps and intelligent ride modes, delivering a truly seamless and premium riding experience. To explore models and current pricing, visit your nearest Ather Experience Centre in Bangalore.

### What is the lowest price of electric scooter?

The lowest price electric scooter from Ather in Bangalore is the Ather Rizta, with an on-road starting price of ₹1,22,572. For those seeking a sportier ride, the Ather 450S also offers a competitive entry point, starting around ₹1,37,835 on-road. Both models offer a premium riding experience with smart features.

### What is the average price of an electric scooter?

The average on-road price for an Ather electric scooter in Bangalore typically falls in the range of ₹1,22,572 to ₹1,55,136. This range primarily covers models like the Ather Rizta (starting around ₹1,22,572) and the Ather 450S (starting around ₹1,37,835). The whole range of models offer a premium riding experience with smart features.

### Which Ather electric scooter has the highest range?

Ather 450X offers a range of 161 km, making it the best range e scooter for daily commutes and long city rides.

### What factors affect electric scooter range?

Range depends on the riding mode, speed and terrain. Being a lightweight electric scooter also helps. Smooth riding ensures you get the highest range.

### Which Ather electric scooter has the highest speed?

Ather 450 Apex is the high speed electric scooter in our lineup, with a top speed of 100 km/h. It is built for riders who seek performance at its peak.

### What factors affect the speed of an electric scooter?

Weight, terrain and riding modes influence the performance of an electric scooter with high speed.

### What colours are available in Ather electric scooters?

Ather Rizta comes in Terracotta Red Super Matte, Pangong Blue Super Matte, Terracotta Red Duo, Pangong Blue Duo, Alphonso Yellow Duo, Cardamom Green Duo, Deccan Grey Duo, Deccan Grey Mono and Siachen White Mono. Ather 450 comes in Hyper Sand, Stealth Blue, True Red, Lunar Grey, Cosmic Black, Space Grey and Still White. Ather 450 Apex comes only in Indium Blue.

### Which is the lightweight electric scooter from Ather?

Ather 450S 2.9 kWh (SLR) and Ather 450X 2.9 kWh (XLR) are the light weight EV scooters from Ather, with a kerb weight of 108 kg.
